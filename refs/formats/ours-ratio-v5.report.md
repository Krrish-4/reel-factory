## Narrative arc

1. Introduce a visual puzzle showing a face with one hidden change.
2. Reveal that the chin changed, not the nose, introducing the concept of holistic facial perception.
3. Provide pixel-level proof of the change using a heatmap.
4. Explain the structural relationship between the nose and chin.
5. Provide a second example using upper blepharoplasty to show how eyelid changes affect eye perception.
6. Explain the rule of facial thirds.
7. Engage the viewer with an interactive physical test (jaw projection) to prove the optical illusion.
8. Introduce the Refina AI tool as a way to preview these holistic changes.
