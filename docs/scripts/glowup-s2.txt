What nose shape do you actually have? Here are the six main types - and how each one changes a whole face. First, the button nose. A short bridge with a softly upturned tip that makes the midface read compact and youthful. Second, the Greek straight nose. A completely flat bridge from brow to tip that gives the side profile sharp, editorial structure. Third, the dorsal curve. A slight ridge along the bridge that adds strong bone character and balances a projected chin. Fourth, the rounded tip. Fuller cartilage at the lower tip, which softens angular cheekbones from the front view. Fifth, narrow sloped - delicate from the front, curved on the side.
