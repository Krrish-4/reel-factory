# "It Isn't the Lips" — FINAL. Explainer told as a confession, one woman to
# another. Audience: women 40-50, scrolling at night, there for entertainment.
# Variants A and B keep two alternative openings; this is the cut to build.
#
# THE OPENING IS A QUESTION, WHICH IS THE CORPUS-NATIVE FORM — 14 of the 21
# references open on one — and it names her in word three. It also does the
# one thing the earlier drafts could not: it gives the viewer a reason to stay
# that is not about her own face. She is curious about someone else for four
# seconds, and beat 2 turns it on her while she is already listening.
#
# THE ORDER IS HOOK -> STORY -> EDUCATION -> OFFER. She is on Instagram for
# entertainment, so the answer to the opening question arrives as the TWIST at
# beat 4, about 20 seconds in, and only after she has asked for it. v1-v3 all
# opened by teaching her geometry, which is a lecture with a story-shaped
# first line, and a woman scrolling at 9:40pm does not stop for it.
#
# BEAT 3 ANNOUNCES THE SUBJECT CHANGE, AND THE QUESTION OPENING IS WHY.
# "What makes Sydney Sweeney's lips look like that?" sets up HER mouth, and
# what we can actually show is our own pair. Cutting silently from her name to
# a different woman is exactly RefinaChanged's worst defect — it swapped who
# its subject was three times in a reel arguing "same face, one thing moved".
# "Different woman. Same idea." costs four words and closes it. Do not remove
# that line to save time; it is the honesty of the whole reel.
#
# SWEENEY IS NAMED, NOT SHOWN, AND THAT KEEPS GATE 1 CLOSED. No licensed
# photograph is needed and no altered image of a named person is generated —
# the latter was already declined once here (RefinaSize, CLAUDE.md). Beat 1
# wants a type card, a phone or a screen. NEVER her face, and never a render
# of it. If someone puts her likeness on screen, the whole gate reopens.
#
# SPOKEN, NOT WRITTEN. Contractions, fragments and "and no," are deliberate.
# Passive voice, jargon ("re-read", "arrive") and written connectives ("which
# is why") are what made v3 read as copy. Read any edit ALOUD: if a woman
# talking to a friend could not say it in one breath, it is wrong for this
# reel.
#
# TONE IS LOAD-BEARING AND THE VOICE IS NOT CHOSEN. `vesta` is calm and
# clinical and will flatten this back into the lecture. Audition for WARMTH
# before synthesising — CLAUDE.md: pick the voice before tuning pacing.
#
# NEVER: an outcome, a treatment recommendation, or a figure about a face.
# Beat 7 says "more isn't better", never "filler isn't the answer" — the
# second is treatment advice, and this reel carries an AI PREVIEW · NOT
# MEDICAL ADVICE chip precisely because it gives none.
#
