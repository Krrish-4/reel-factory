# THE ARGUMENT: the eye does not read a face as a list of features, it reads
# each feature against the ones around it. So the pair in the hook is one whose
# changed pixels are provably nowhere near the feature the viewer will point at.
# `gen/score.py`'s concentration measurement is not just a screen here — it is
# the reel's evidence, and the proof beat puts it on screen.
#
# WHAT IT NEVER DOES: claim an outcome, cite a study, or name a number about a
# face. Every assertion is either a photographic fact about the pair on screen
# or something the viewer can reproduce with their own jaw in the next ten
# seconds. The faces are AI renders and are labelled as AI renders on screen.
#
# TWO FACES, AND THE SECOND ONE IS ANNOUNCED. Beat 5 says "another face" out
# loud. The first cut of RefinaChanged silently changed who the subject was and
# that was its worst defect; a second subject is fine, a silent one is not.
#
