## What the reel is

A live-action clinic reel, not a motion-graphics explainer. One woman, two real shots, then three
AI "after" previews in identical framing, each labelled with a procedure combo.

| B | 3.50–7.57 | Wider. Practitioner at frame-left, head out of frame, taupe polo, holds a black tablet in front of her (we see its back). **Lilac screen glow on her face.** She looks down at it and breaks into a smile ~5.5s. | "Refina created the best and most subtle procedure outlooks for her." / "Which is the best?" |

| E | 15.17–19.06 | Preview 3. | "3) Laser Treatment + Deep Lift + Full Bleph" + same CTA |

C→D→E swaps are invisible to a whole-frame cut detector (only the face changes); they were
found on the face region. Text is white rounded boxes, black sans, centred at ~65–80% frame
height. Audio: continuous music, no speech (Gemini says a piano/pop "Vienna" cover — unverified;
use an Instagram library track, not a copyrighted one).

## Step 0 — cast her once (image, before any video)

Every clip must show the same woman, so generate a reference still first and use it as the
ingredient/first frame for every clip. Do **not** lift her face from the source reel — it is
someone else's content.

> Photorealistic portrait, vertical 9:16. A 65-year-old woman seated in a white leather
> treatment chair in a bright, white aesthetic clinic. Short chin-length brown bob with grey
> streaks, side parting, tucked behind one ear. Natural mature skin: fine lines around the eyes,
> nasolabial folds, soft jowls, light crepiness on the neck. No makeup visible. Heather-grey
> long-sleeve henley with a button placket. Background: white wall shelves holding skincare bottles
> (white and navy), a steel instrument tray, softly out of focus. Soft, even, neutral daylight
> clinic lighting. Medium close-up, eye level, head and shoulders. iPhone footage look. No text.

The woman's visible ageing is what the "after" argument rests on. Keep it in the reference; a
smoothed hero leaves the previews nothing to change.

## Section 3 — the Refina card (7.6–19.1, 11.5s)

**Background: Flow clip 3 (generate 8s, use 8s + hold/loop to 11.5s, or generate two).**
> [ingredient: hero still] Vertical 9:16, locked-off medium close-up, same woman, same chair and
> clinic. She sits calmly, small natural breathing movement, occasional blink, looking slightly
> down as if watching something. No speaking. No text.

It goes under the card dimmed/blurred, so it only has to be continuous and on-model.

**Inside the card: a 3D-scan turntable of HER, not of one of the existing scan clips.**
The existing clips (`Woman_rotating_for_3D_scan_*`, `Woman_3D_scan_camera_rotation_*`,
`Woman_rotating_180_degrees_camera_*`, 2026-09-28) are four different women. Use them as the
**style template** — white seamless, black top, profile→front→profile, locked camera — and
generate her turntable from the hero still:

> [ingredient: hero still] Studio 3D-scan reference video. Plain white seamless background, flat
> soft even lighting with no shadows. The same 65-year-old woman, hair tucked behind her ears,
> wearing a plain black V-neck top, neutral expression, mouth closed. She rotates slowly and
> evenly from full left profile, through front-facing, to full right profile, as if on a
> turntable. Camera locked, head and shoulders centred, constant speed. No text, no watermark.

**The three options.** The source shows three different "after" faces. Two ways to do it:

1. **(Recommended) One turntable per option, from real Refina output.** Run her hero still through
   the Refina app for each combo, then generate a turntable from each Refina preview. The card
   says "Made by Refina", so what is inside it has to actually come from Refina — a Flow-invented
   "after" labelled as Refina's would be a false claim about the product.
2. One turntable of her unchanged, with the three option labels switching over it. Cheaper, but the
   card then shows no result at all.

**Timing inside the card**, matched to the source's 3.8s per option:

| option | ours | label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7.6–11.4 | 1) RF Skin Sculpt + Filler |
| 2 | 11.4–15.2 | 2) SMAS Lift + Full Bleph + Buccal |
| 3 | 15.2–19.1 | 3) Laser Treatment + Deep Lift + Full Bleph |

A 10s turntable does not fit 3.8s. Use the front-weighted half of each (profile → front → ¾,
roughly 2.5–8.5s of the clip) at ~1.6x, so each option reads as one sweep and the face is
frontal at the moment its label lands. Cut on the frontal frame so the swap between options reads
as a change of result, not a jump in angle.

**Slot for Claude Design** (1080x1920 frame):

  caption zone
- turntable window inside it: ~4:5, top of card

  does
